Privacy Policy

Short version: MenuMatch doesn't collect your data. It stays on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage.

Last updated November 19, 2026

What stays on your devices

  • Menu photos and PDFs you scan or import, and the menus read from them.
  • Your profile: diets, allergies and intolerances, dislikes, favourite cuisines, flavours and nutrition goals.
  • What you ordered, ratings and notes, and the taste preferences learned from them (you can turn this off or reset it).

On each device this data is protected by iOS data protection. We have no server, so we never receive it.

iCloud sync

If iCloud is on for MenuMatch, your profile, menus, page photos and ratings sync between your iPhone and iPad through your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). Only your Apple Account can read it; we can't. You can turn sync off in Profile → iCloud. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

How menus are read

Text recognition, translation and dish explanations run on your device with Apple's built-in frameworks (Vision, Translation and, where available, Apple Intelligence on-device models). Your allergy information is never sent to any model or server.

Dish photos

When dish photos are enabled, MenuMatch sends the dish name and preparation to Openverse to find freely licensed reference photos. The lookup does not send your menu images, profile or allergy information. Photo details show the creator, source and license. You can turn photos off in Profile. See the Openverse privacy policy. Earlier builds may use Wikimedia for photo lookups. See the Wikimedia privacy policy.

Location and restaurant reminders (optional)

Only if you turn on restaurant reminders, MenuMatch asks for your location while you use the app when you scan a menu. It uses Apple Maps to find the restaurant you're at, saves the restaurant's name and the scan location with that menu, and asks iOS to show one reminder when you leave, so you can note what you ordered. MenuMatch doesn't track your location in the background and doesn't send it to us. You can turn reminders off any time.

Reviews and videos

Buttons for YouTube, TikTok, Google and Maps open those services with a search for the dish or restaurant, only when you tap them. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to photograph menus, when you tap Scan.
  • Photos: the system photo picker, so MenuMatch only sees the photos you choose.
  • Location (While Using) and Notifications: only for the optional restaurant reminder.

What we don't do

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs, and no sharing or selling of data.

Deleting your data

Delete a single menu in the Menus tab, or everything with Delete Everything in the Profile tab. That also removes the copies in your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data on that device.

Children

MenuMatch is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
