Privacy Policy

Short version: MenuMatch does not collect any data. Everything stays on your device.

Last updated October 25, 2026

What stays on your device

  • Menu photos and PDFs you scan or import, and the menus read from them.
  • Your profile: diets, allergies and intolerances, dislikes, favourite cuisines, flavours and nutrition goals.
  • Saved dishes, notes and ratings, and the taste preferences learned from them (you can turn this off or reset it).

This data is stored only on your device, protected by iOS data protection. We have no server, so we never receive it. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on.

How menus are read

Text recognition, translation and dish explanations run on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks (Vision, Translation and, where available, Apple Intelligence on-device models). If iOS needs to download a translation language, that download is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Your allergy information is never sent to any model or server.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to photograph menus, and only when you tap Scan.
  • Photos: MenuMatch uses the system photo picker, so it only sees the photos you choose.

What we don't do

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs, and no sharing or selling of data.

Deleting your data

Delete a single menu in the Menus tab, or everything with Delete Everything in the Profile tab. Deleting the app removes all its data.

Children

MenuMatch is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
