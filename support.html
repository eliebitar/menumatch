Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to info@eb-net.org. We usually answer within two working days.

Frequently asked questions

Can MenuMatch tell me a dish is safe for my allergy?

No. A menu photo can't prove that a dish is free of an allergen or that there is no cross-contact in the kitchen. MenuMatch shows what the menu declares, what is typical for the dish and what is unknown, and helps you ask staff. Always confirm with the restaurant.

What do the labels next to each ingredient mean?

On the menu: printed on this menu. Typical: common in traditional recipes for this dish, not confirmed for this restaurant. Estimate: worked out from the description. Unknown: not enough information.

Why are calories shown as a range?

Restaurants rarely publish recipes or portion sizes, so MenuMatch shows a range and says what it assumed. You can change the portion size on the dish page.

The text wasn't read correctly.

Take the photo straight on, in good light, one page at a time. You can retake a single page before reading it, and correct any dish name or price afterwards.

Translation doesn't appear.

MenuMatch uses Apple's on-device translation. If your iPhone hasn't downloaded the language yet, iOS asks to download it once. Dish explanations work without it.

How do I import a PDF or a photo from another app?

Tap Files or Photos on the Scan tab, or use the Share button in Safari, Mail, Files or Photos and choose MenuMatch.

Which languages are supported?

The app is in English and German. Menus in German and English are understood best; French, Italian and Spanish menus are also recognised, and staff cards exist in those five languages.

How do I delete my data?

Swipe to delete a single menu in the Menus tab, or use Delete Everything in the Profile tab. Deleting the app removes everything too.

MenuMatch provides information, not medical advice.
